# Wallet Security

Your wallet in Cirata is managed securely so you never handle private keys or seed phrases.

## How Your Wallet Works

- Wallets are provided through **Circle Developer-Controlled Wallets**, so you do not manage a seed phrase or browser extension.
- Your wallet is created automatically when you log in with Google. No manual setup is needed.
- The wallet address is displayed in the Treasury and Settings pages for receiving funds.

## Network

- Cirata runs on **Arc Testnet**.
- Gas fees on Arc are paid with USDC.

## Stay Safe

- Always double-check the recipient address before sending.
- Send a small test amount first when sending to a new address.
- Never share access to your Google account.
- Log out on shared or public devices.
- Set Spending Limits to cap individual transfers.
- Verify transactions on the Arc block explorer after sending.

## Important Notes

- Funds on Arc Testnet **have no real value**. Do not treat testnet tokens as real assets.
- Do not send assets from other networks to your Cirata address — this may result in loss of funds.
- Cirata is in a development phase. Always verify transactions on [Arc Explorer](https://testnet.arcscan.app/) before considering them final.

<Tip>
  You can view your wallet on the Arc Testnet block explorer from the Treasury or Settings page.
</Tip>
