# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Cirata?

Cirata is a USDC/EURC treasury dashboard on Arc Testnet. Wallets are automatically created on Google login.

## Is MetaMask required?

No. Wallets are managed server-side via Circle. You don't need to install a wallet extension.

## Are funds real?

No. All funds on Arc Testnet have no real value.

## Why is my balance 0?

1. Make sure you're logged in
2. Wait 1–3 seconds after login (wallet provisioning)
3. Refresh the page
4. Check if there are incoming transactions on Arc Explorer

## How to export data?

Open the **Activity** menu, then click **Export CSV**, **Export Excel**, or **Export PDF**.

## What is voice command for?

Filling in the transfer form via voice. Press the microphone button on the Transfer page.

## Who can use it?

Anyone with a Google account. A wallet is automatically created per email.

## How to receive funds?

Open Treasury → **Show QR for Receiving**, then share the address or QR code with the sender. Make sure the sender uses Arc Testnet.
