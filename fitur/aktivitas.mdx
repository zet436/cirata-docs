# Activity

The **Activity** page shows your full transaction history for your Circle wallet on Arc Testnet.

## Transaction History

Each entry displays:

- **Type** — Send (TRANSFER) or Receive.
- **Amount** — Token value.
- **Token** — USDC or EURC with logo.
- **Status** — Success, Failed, or pending state.
- **Date** — Creation timestamp.
- **Direction** — Inbound or Outbound.
- **Destination / Source** — The other party's address.
- **Category** — User-assigned tag (if set).

## Filters

Narrow your history with the filter bar:

- **Token** — All, USDC, or EURC.
- **Direction** — All, Inbound, or Outbound.
- **Category** — All or a specific category found in your transactions.
- **Search** — Free-text search by address or token symbol.

<Tip>
  Filters combine. For example, search "0x1234" with token USDC and direction Outbound to find one specific transfer.
</Tip>

## Views

Toggle between two tabs at the top:

- **Transactions** — Your on-chain transfer history.
- **Audit Log** — Internal audit events including pending approvals.

## Export

Three export options are available:

- **Export CSV** — Downloads a standard CSV file.
- **Export Excel** — Downloads a CSV with BOM so Excel opens it correctly.
- **Export PDF** — Opens the browser print dialog for PDF export.

## Empty State

If no transactions exist yet, the page shows "No activity data available." Make your first transfer to populate the list.
