# Transfer

## Transfer Steps

1. Open the **Transfer** menu
2. Select token: **USDC** or **EURC**
3. Select a contact from the Address Book (optional) or enter the recipient address `0x...`
4. Enter the amount (click **MAX** to send all balance)
5. Fill in category/memo (optional)
6. Click **Send**
7. Confirm in the modal that appears

## Additional Features

- **Voice Command** — Voice input to auto-fill the transfer form.
- **Address Book** — Save favorite addresses in Treasury → Address Book.
- **Spending Limits** — Set spending limits in Treasury → Spending Limits.

## Validation

- Recipient address must be valid (format `0x` + 40 hex characters).
- Amount must be > 0 and not exceed balance.
- Login required to send.

> ⚠️ Transactions are performed on **Arc Testnet**. Testnet funds have no real value.
