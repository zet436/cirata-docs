# Transfer

The **Transfer** page lets you send USDC or EURC to any valid 0x address on Arc Testnet.

## Transfer Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select token">
    Choose **USDC** or **EURC** using the toggle buttons at the top.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter destination">
    Type a valid 0x address, or pick one from the **Address Book** dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount">
    Type the amount. Click **MAX** to send your entire balance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add details (optional)">
    Select a category and write a memo for your records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send and confirm">
    Click **Send**, review the summary modal, and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Summary Panel

The right-hand panel shows a live summary before you send:

- **From** — Your wallet address.
- **To** — Destination address.
- **Amount** — Token and value.
- **Network** — Arc Testnet.
- **Total** — Final amount.

## Additional Features

- **Voice Command** — Use the microphone button to fill the form by speaking.
- **Address Book** — Save and reuse recipient addresses.
- **Spending Limits** — Set per-token caps to prevent overspending.

## Validation Rules

- Destination address must match the format `0x` followed by 40 hex characters.
- Amount must be greater than 0.
- Amount must not exceed your available balance.
- You must be logged in to send.

<Tip>
  The Send button stays disabled until all fields are valid.
</Tip>
