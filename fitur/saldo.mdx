# Balance & Treasury

The **Treasury** page is your financial overview. It shows your holdings, cash flow, and wallet details in one place.

## Holdings

A table listing each asset you hold:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Asset | Token name and logo (USDC, EURC) |
| Balance | Current on-chain balance |
| Share % | Percentage of total portfolio |
| Inflow | Total received |
| Outflow | Total sent |
| Network | Arc Testnet |
| Actions | Send or Receive |

## Summary Cards

Three cards at the top give a quick snapshot:

- **Assets Held** — Number of distinct tokens.
- **Total Inflow** — Sum of all inbound transfers.
- **Total Outflow** — Sum of all outbound transfers.

## Charts

Three visualizations help you understand your treasury over time:

- **Allocation (pie)** — Distribution of USDC vs EURC by amount.
- **Cash Flow (bar)** — Inbound vs outbound totals.
- **Treasury Performance (line)** — Balance history reconstructed from transactions.

<Tip>
  Hover over chart data points for exact values.
</Tip>

## Wallet Account

The Wallet Account panel shows:

- **Network Address** — Your 0x wallet address with a copy button.
- **Linked Email** — The Google email tied to this wallet.
- **Status** — Active / Arc Live indicator.
- **Show QR for Receiving** — Displays a QR code and address for receiving funds.
- **View on Explorer** — Opens your address on the Arc Testnet block explorer.

## Receive Assets

1. Click **Show QR for Receiving**.
2. A QR code and your wallet address appear.
3. Share the address or QR with the sender.

<Warning>
  Make sure the sender uses the **Arc Testnet** network. Sending from other networks may result in loss of funds.
</Warning>

## Treasury Controls

Three sub-sections are accessible from the Treasury page:

- **Spending Limits** — Set per-token transfer caps.
- **Address Book** — Save and manage recipient addresses.
- **Approval Policy** — Shows the active approval policy status.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download your transaction history as a CSV file.
