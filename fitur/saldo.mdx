# Balance & Treasury

## Treasury Page

The **Treasury** menu displays:

- **Holdings** — Real-time USDC and EURC balances from on-chain.
- **Total Inflow** — Total incoming funds.
- **Total Outflow** — Total outgoing funds.
- **Charts** — Allocation (pie), Cash Flow (bar), Performance (line).
- **Wallet Account** — Wallet address, connected email, status.
- **Receive** — QR code and address for receiving funds.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download transaction history as CSV.

## Receive Assets

1. Click **Show QR for Receiving**
2. The QR code and wallet address will appear
3. Share the address or QR with the sender

> ⚠️ Make sure the sender uses the **Arc Testnet** network. Sending from other networks may cause loss of funds.
