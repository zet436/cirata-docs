# Quick Start

Get up and running in five steps.

## Before You Start

- A Google account.
- A modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
- No wallet extension required.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in via Google">
    Open Cirata and click **Sign in with Google**. Select your Google account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for wallet creation">
    The system auto-creates a Circle Developer-Controlled wallet for your email. This takes 1–3 seconds. You will see the dashboard once the wallet is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your balance">
    Open the **Treasury** menu. Your USDC and EURC balances are shown in real time from the Arc Testnet chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send tokens">
    Open the **Transfer** menu. Select a token (USDC or EURC), enter a destination address, enter an amount, and click **Send**. Confirm in the modal that appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review history">
    Open the **Activity** menu to see all transactions. You can filter by token, direction, and category, and export to CSV, Excel, or PDF.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Tips

<Tip>
  Click **MAX** on the Transfer page to send your entire balance.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Use the Address Book (Treasury → Address Book) to save frequently used recipient addresses.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Set Spending Limits (Treasury → Spending Limits) to cap how much can be sent per transaction.
</Tip>
