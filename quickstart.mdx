# Quickstart

## Step 1: Login

1. Open [Cirata](http://localhost:3000)
2. Click **Sign in with Google**
3. Select your Google account

## Step 2: Automatic Wallet

After login, the system automatically creates a Circle Developer-Controlled wallet on Arc Testnet. This process takes 1–3 seconds.

## Step 3: Check Balance

Open the **Treasury** menu to view your USDC and EURC balances.

## Step 4: Transfer

1. Open the **Transfer** menu
2. Select token (USDC/EURC)
3. Enter the recipient address (0x...)
4. Enter the amount
5. Click **Send**, then confirm

## Step 5: View History

Open the **Activity** menu to view transaction history. You can filter by token, direction (inbound/outbound), and category.

> ⚠️ Cirata runs on **Arc Testnet**. Displayed funds have no real value.
