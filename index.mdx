# Cirata

Cirata is a web treasury management app for **USDC** and **EURC** on **Arc Testnet**. You log in with Google, and a wallet is created for you automatically — no MetaMask, no seed phrase, no browser extension.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in via Google">
    Open Cirata and click **Sign in with Google**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wallet is created">
    The system auto-creates a Circle Developer-Controlled wallet for your email. This takes 1–3 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the dashboard">
    Check your balance, send tokens, and review your transaction history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  All wallets are managed server-side. You never handle private keys or seed phrases.
</Tip>

## Who Is It For

- Individuals who want to test USDC/EURC transfers on Arc Testnet.
- Teams that need a simple treasury view without wallet extensions.
- Developers exploring Circle Developer-Controlled Wallets.
